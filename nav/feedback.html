Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders in Lagos and Abuja arrive in 1–3 working days. Other cities typically take 3–7 working days.

Can I return a pair?

Yes. Unworn items can be returned within 7 days. Start a return from Track Order or send us a message.

How do sizes work?

We list EU sizes on every product. If you are between sizes, go up one — or ask the retailer in chat.

How do I pay?

Checkout uses a secure payment link. You will get an email receipt after a successful payment.

Contact us

We read every message. For orders, use Track Order. For everything else, send the form on this page or reach us here:

  • Email hello@sweetfeet.ng
  • Hours Mon–Sat, 9am – 6pm WAT
  • Chat Message a retailer from any product, or use Messages after you log in.

Need support?

Tell us what went wrong and we will follow up. Include your order ID if you have one.

  • Payment charged twice
  • Wrong size or damaged item
  • Delivery delay
  • Account or login issues
